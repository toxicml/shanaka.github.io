Intelligent Road-Sign Line-Following Robot

PID line following · Hybrid sign recognition · Obstacle handling

An intelligent mobile robot that follows a black line with an 8-IR PID controller, adapts speed to limit signs, and makes junction decisions from ordered road-sign detection. Vision combines EasyOCR for numeric speed signs (20, 100) with a Random-Forest classifier on HOG+HSV features for symbolic signs (BUS STOP, P, TRAIN), fusing results to trigger precise pause, stop, and turn actions. A companion app enables manual, line-follow, and obstacle-assist modes.

MEMS Single-Cell Injection Platform

Electrostatic injector · Microgripper · Force feedback

A monolithic MEMS device integrating an electrostatic comb-drive injector, dual-arm microgripper, and piezoresistive force sensors for high-precision single-cell injection. COMSOL simulations validated safe forces (10–20 µN), strokes exceeding 5 µm, and stable gripping dynamics above 3 kHz. The work has been published as a peer-reviewed paper, underscoring its biomedical relevance.
