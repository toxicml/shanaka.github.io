Overview

An IoT-based monitoring station that acquires ambient temperature, relative humidity, solar irradiance, light intensity, wind speed & direction, and rainfall. The system blends commercial sensors with custom instruments, an ultrasonic anemometer for wind, a tipping-bucket mechanism for rainfall, and a bidirectional panel for irradiance, to deliver accurate, real-time environmental data. Signals are conditioned, calibrated, and streamed to a backend for visualization on dedicated web and mobile applications, enabling timely insights and decision support.

Skills Involved

  • IoT firmware & data acquisition
  • Signal conditioning & sensor calibration
  • Commercial + custom sensor integration
  • Real-time telemetry & data handling
  • Web & mobile UI for visualization
  • Hardware assembly & enclosure design
